It is what I hope I would have access to, 10 years ago.

Agile grew as a response to waterfall, as software was built in different institutional and market settings, and as the technical tools kept changing.

The technical solutions exist, we know how to build good quality software in a timely manner.

It is the social conditions that determine what will happen in the end. That is my explanation for the disillusionment expressed by some of the manifesto signatories.
(https://builtin.com/software-engineering-perspectives/kent-beck-geeks-gusto-globalization)

BuiltIn:You signed the Agile Manifesto almost 20 years ago. How do you feel about agile now?
Kent Beck: It’s a devastated wasteland. The life has been sucked out of it. It’s a few religious rituals carried out by people who don’t understand the purpose that those rituals were intended to serve in the first place.
